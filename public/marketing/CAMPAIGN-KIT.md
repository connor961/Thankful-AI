### One-liner
Thankful helps you say thank you in a way that actually feels like you — it tracks every gift and giver, and lets you record, upload, or type, then draft with AI, work hybrid, or write every word yourself.

### Short description (≈50 words)
Thankful is the flexible thank-you-note app for life's big gift moments. It remembers who gave what and what made it meaningful, then meets you where you are: record the gift-opening, upload a list, or type it in; let AI draft, collaborate, or write it all yourself. Free to start.

### Long description (≈120 words)
After a wedding, shower, or birthday, gratitude is easy to feel and hard to keep up with — dozens of gifts, dozens of names, and a blank stack of cards. Thankful removes that friction. It keeps track of every gift, who gave it, and the detail that made it special, so nothing slips through the cracks.

From there, it's entirely your call. Capture the moment by recording and transcribing, uploading a list you already have, or typing as you go. Write with AI as your first draft, work side by side with it, or write every word yourself. Finish by email, a printed fold-out card, a mailable postcard, or peel-and-stick address labels.

The gratitude was always yours. Thankful just makes it doable.
