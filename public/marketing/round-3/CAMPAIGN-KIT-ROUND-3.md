# Thankful — Campaign Kit (Round 3 · Weeks 5–8)

The third month of Instagram assets, continuing the same brand system as
Rounds 1–2. Sixteen new graphics, files **55–70**, in `public/marketing/round-3/`.

See `INSTAGRAM-CALENDAR-ROUND-3.md` for the day-by-day posting schedule and
ready-to-paste captions.

---

## The story this month
Rounds 1–2 introduced Thankful, handled "it's not too late," and covered
occasions and the how-it-works. Round 3 goes deeper on **motivation and
conversion** across four weeks:

1. **Week 5 — Why it matters.** The emotional case for gratitude: be the person
   who says it, and the quiet upside for the writer.
2. **Week 6 — The season of thanks.** Timely fall → holiday hook: get ahead of
   the gift avalanche before it starts.
3. **Week 7 — It really is this easy.** Knock down objections ("I'm not a
   writer") and show the real product (capture any way, print + mail).
4. **Week 8 — Start today.** Lower the bar, hand over a plan, create momentum
   ("send one note tonight").

## Brand system (unchanged from Rounds 1–2)
- **Palette**
  - Cream background `#fbf1e8`, card surface `#fefbf7`
  - Ink (near-brown) text `#3a231f`
  - Coral (primary) `#c84b5b`, soft coral `#f7d9d5`
  - Gold accent `#ebc08b`
  - Muted body text `#6f5f58`
- **Type**
  - Display / headlines: **Fraunces** (900 weight, tight tracking; second line often coral italic)
  - Body / labels: **Nunito** (600–800)
  - Handwritten accent / tagline: **Caveat**
- **Signatures**
  - Wordmark: coral dot + "Thankful" in Fraunces
  - Coral pill CTA, uppercase letter-spaced kicker
  - Fraunces headline with one italic coral accent line
  - Script tagline bottom-right on hero cards

## How these were made
Rendered as HTML/CSS at exact pixel dimensions and screenshotted for crisp,
on-brand typography — identical pipeline to Rounds 1–2. The temporary generator
(`public/_mktg-gen3/`) and dev save-bridge (`app/api/mktg-save/`) were removed
after export to keep the app clean; only the PNGs and these docs remain. To
regenerate or tweak wording later, restore the generator from git history
(commit that added `public/_mktg-gen3/index.html`) and re-render.

## Usage tips
- Point your bio link at signup; every CTA says "link in bio" or "get started free."
- Stories 62, 66, 70 are built for interactive stickers (poll + link).
- 58 and 68 are the most "save-worthy" — good for reach.
- Mix in evergreen Round 1–2 quotes/tips on quiet days for a fuller grid.
