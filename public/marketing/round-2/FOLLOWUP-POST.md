## Instagram caption

Two weeks ago we launched. Then you started telling us things. 🙏

The most useful piece of feedback wasn't a compliment. It was this, over and
over, in different words:

"I don't actually want AI to write it for me."

Fair. If gratitude gets fully automated, it stops being gratitude.

So we spent two weeks making Thankful less of an AI writing tool and more of a
gratitude system — where AI is one option, not the whole point.

What's new:

✍️ Write them yourself. Blank card, your words, no AI at all.
🎚️ Or start from a draft and make it yours — set the tone, edit freely.
✅ Track what's done. The hard part isn't writing the notes, it's remembering
who you still owe.
🖨️ Print, fold and actually mail them — real cards, postcards, address labels.
📬 Or send by email when that's the right call.

It used to be "let AI write your thank-you notes." Now it's simpler: the notes
get written and sent — however you want to do that.

Thank you to everyone who tried it and told us what was missing. Keep it
coming. 🤎

Still free to start — 20 notes, no card required. Link in bio.

🌐 thankfulai.org
