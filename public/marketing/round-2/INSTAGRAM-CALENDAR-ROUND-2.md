# Thankful — Instagram Calendar, Round 2 (Weeks 5–8)

A 4-week posting plan built around the Round 2 assets. Five posts per week
(Mon/Tue/Wed/Fri + a weekend Story), tuned so each week has one hook, a couple
of value posts, and one save-worthy or social-proof beat.

- Post feed content ~9am or ~6pm local.
- Stories any time; weekends are great for polls.
- Every post drives to `/launch`. Keep "20 free notes, no card, no monthly
  reset" in caption or first comment.
- Engage in the first 30 min after posting — it's the biggest free lever you
  have.

---

**Weekly focus:** reactivation. Pin the hero post; reply to every "omg this is
me" comment with encouragement + link.

## REUSE + REMIX IDEAS

- **Carousels:** bundle the 5 Week-6 occasion cards into one "unexpected
  thank-yous" carousel; bundle the 3 quote cards into a testimonial carousel.
- **Reels:** film a 15s "watch me clear my thank-you pile in 5 minutes"
  screen-record using the app; use `41-quote-worth-it` as the cover.
- **Cross-post:** every square works as-is on Facebook, LinkedIn (great for
  the client/donor/retirement cards), and Pinterest (the two tip verticals
  especially).
- **First comment:** drop the `/launch` link + free-tier line as the first
  comment on every feed post to keep captions clean.
