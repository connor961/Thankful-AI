# Thankful — Marketing Kit, Round 2

A second wave of 22 ready-to-post graphics + captions, plus a fresh 4-week
content calendar (Weeks 5–8). This round is designed to run *after* Round 1 —
it opens up new occasions, digs into writing craft, and turns gratitude into a
repeatable habit, rather than repeating the "what is Thankful" intro beats.

- **Brand:** Thankful — the easiest way to write, print, and mail real
  thank-you notes.
- **Voice:** warm, encouraging, never guilt-trippy. We remove friction and
  shame from a task people already feel behind on.
- **Palette:** cream `#FBF1E8`, deep brown ink `#3A231F`, coral `#C84B5B`,
  gold `#EBC08B`.
- **Standing CTA:** *Start free · link in bio* (20 free notes, no card, no
  monthly reset).
- **Landing page:** send campaign traffic to `/launch`.

Files live alongside this document in `/public/marketing/round-2/`. Dimensions
are baked into each filename.

---

## THE FOUR THEMES

- **Week 5 — Late isn't too late.** Permission + gentle urgency. The note you
  never sent is still worth sending.
- **Week 6 — Beyond the wedding.** New occasions: sympathy, teachers,
  retirement, clients, donors.
- **Week 7 — Make it personal.** Writing craft — tone, openers, what to say
  when a gift was… a choice.
- **Week 8 — Your gratitude system.** Habit-building, multi-event, reminders,
  keeping gratitude in rotation year-round.
