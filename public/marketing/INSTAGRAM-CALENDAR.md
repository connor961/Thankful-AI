## How to actually post it (recommended setup)

You said you're not sure which tool to use. Here's the simplest free path:

1. **Meta Business Suite** (business.facebook.com) — free, official, schedules
   Instagram + Facebook together. Best choice if you'll also post to your FB page.
   - Convert your IG to a **Professional account** (free): Settings → Account type.
   - Link it to a Facebook Page, then schedule from Business Suite → Planner.
2. **Post from your phone** — if you'd rather not schedule, set 3 weekly phone
   reminders (Mon/Wed/Fri, ~11am or ~7pm). Copy the caption from this file,
   attach the graphic, paste the first comment after posting.

**Best times** (general starting point — check your own Insights after week 1):
Weekdays **11am–1pm** or **7–9pm** local. Post consistently at the same times.

**Every post gets:**
- The caption (already written below)
- A **first comment** with the hashtags (keeps the caption clean)
- Alt text (Advanced settings → Write alt text) for accessibility + reach
